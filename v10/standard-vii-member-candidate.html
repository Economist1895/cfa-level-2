Standard VII: Responsibilities as a CFA Institute Member or CFA Candidate

In 30 seconds

  • VII(A): don't compromise CFA Institute's reputation or the integrity, validity or security of its programs: no cheating, no breaking test rules, no leaking exam content, no misusing volunteer roles.
  • You may criticise CFA Institute and say whether the exam felt hard; you may not reveal topics or formulas tested or not tested.
  • VII(B): don't misrepresent or exaggerate membership, the charter or candidacy. Facts are fine; claims of superior performance are not.
  • No "partial" designation ("CFA Level II"), no "CFA, expected 20XX". Lapsed membership → stop using the designation.

01Conduct in CFA Institute Programs

VII(A)

Conduct as Participants in CFA Institute Programs

Don't engage in conduct that compromises the reputation or integrity of CFA Institute or the CFA designation, or the integrity, validity or security of CFA Institute programs.

  • Applies to any CFA Institute program (not just the CFA exam), but only to people already bound by the Code (members and candidates).
  • Prohibited: giving or receiving help (cheating); breaking testing rules (any breach = VII(A)); leaking confidential exam info; dodging security; misusing a CFA Institute association for personal or business gain; misrepresenting info to CFA Institute (e.g. a fake emergency deferral request, which is also I(C)).
  • Examples: bringing written notes into the test centre, looking at other screens, disruptive conduct.

Confidential exam information

  • Can't disclose: specific question details, broad topic areas or formulas tested or not tested, until CFA Institute releases them.
  • Can: discuss non-confidential material in study groups; use outside prep providers; say the exam was hard or how you think you did; criticise CFA Institute's policies.
  • Online forums are monitored; reporting suspected leaks is encouraged.

Volunteers

  • Graders, question writers and committee members must not reveal questions, deliberations or scoring information.
  • Using a volunteer role (e.g. a GIPS committee seat) to win clients or claim a business edge → violation.

02Reference to CFA Institute & the Designation

VII(B)

Reference to CFA Institute, the CFA Designation, and the CFA Program

When referring to CFA Institute, membership, the designation or candidacy, don't misrepresent or exaggerate what they mean or imply.

  • OK: facts about the benefits, rigour and ethical commitment; claims of relative merit framed as opinion. Non-opinion statements must be factual.
  • Not OK: implying superior performance or competence because of the charter.
  • Covers every medium: business cards, letterhead, bios, LinkedIn, email signatures, websites, CVs, spoken statements.
  • Pseudonyms: an anonymous profile name must not carry "CFA" ("Expert, CFA" = violation).

Membership & the charter

  • Annual requirements: sign the Membership Agreement, file the Professional Conduct Statement, pay dues.
  • Miss one → no longer a member and lose the right to use the CFA designation until reinstated; remove all references. May say you were a member or charterholder in the past.
  • Charter = right to use the designation, granted by CFA Institute; keeping it requires ongoing membership.

Candidacy

  • A candidate is accepted and enrolled for an exam (or awaiting results). Declining to sit ends candidacy until re-enrolled.
  • May state levels passed and that you're a candidate. Must not imply a partial designation or cite an expected completion date.
  • Passing three levels in consecutive attempts is a fact you may state; linking it to superior ability is not.

💡
Practice: brief legal, compliance, PR and marketing on VII(B), and use firm templates for references to CFA status.

03Exam traps

⚠️
  • Posting that "inventory and tax analysis were on the exam" → VII(A) violation; complaining that the exam was hard → fine.
  • Criticising CFA Institute policy is allowed; using outside prep courses is allowed.
  • Study group reconstructing specific exam questions after the exam → violation.
  • Business card "CFA Level II" → violation; "completed Levels I and II of the CFA Program" → fine.
  • Didn't pay dues or file the PCS → can't use "CFA", even if the exams were passed years ago.
  • "All our principals passed on the first try" is fine only if true and not linked to performance.
  • Fake deferral request → VII(A) and I(C).
  • Standard VII says nothing about loyalty or supervision (those are III, IV).
