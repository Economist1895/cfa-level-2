Pricing and Valuation of Forward Commitments

In 30 seconds

  • Pricing = finding the fixed price/rate that makes the contract worth zero at initiation. Valuation = what an existing contract is worth later.
  • Carry arbitrage: forward price = FV of spot, plus carry costs, minus carry benefits. If the market price differs, buy the cheap side and sell the rich side.
  • Forward value = PV of (new forward price − original forward price). Futures value resets to 0 after each daily mark-to-market.
  • FRA rate = implied forward rate from two spot MRRs. Settlement is paid at FRA expiry, so it is discounted.
  • Swaps = a pair of bonds. Swap rate = (1 − last PV factor) / ΣPV factors. Swap value = NA × Δfixed rate × ΣPV of remaining payments.

01No-arbitrage & carry arbitrage

  • Arbitrageur's two rules: no own money and no price risk. Law of one price: same future cash flows → same price today.
  • Assumptions: replicating instruments are investable, no frictions, short selling with full use of proceeds, borrow/lend at a known risk-free rate.
  • Convergence: at expiry FT​=fT​=ST​. Before expiry, forward and futures prices are treated as equal.

03Interest rate forwards (FRAs)

  • Long FRA = pay fixed, receive floating → gains when rates rise. Hedge for a future borrower. Short FRA = receive fixed (future lender).
  • "h × T" in months: a 3 × 9 FRA expires in 3 months on a 6-month MRR. It replicates long a 9-month deposit and short a 3-month deposit.
  • MRR is add-on, ACT/360: interest = NA × Lm × tm, with tm = m/360.
  • FRAs are advanced set, advanced settled: the rate is set at h and paid at h, so it is discounted. Swaps and rate options are settled in arrears.

04Equity & fixed-income futures

  • Equity index: use F2 with CB = dividend yield. If dividend yield > r, the futures price is below spot.
  • Bonds quote clean (B, no accrued interest). You pay the full price S = B + AI. Futures settle against the clean price.
  • Several bonds are deliverable. The conversion factor (CF) scales each bond to a standard coupon (e.g. 6%). The seller delivers the cheapest-to-deliver bond.

05Interest rate swaps

  • A swap hedges many periods (an FRA hedges one). Floating rate is advanced set, settled in arrears; payments are netted.
  • Receive-fixed = long a fixed-rate bond + short a floating-rate bond. Pay-fixed = long floater + short fixed bond → gains when rates rise.
  • Fixed-rate issuer + receive-fixed swap = synthetic floating-rate bond.
  • A floater is worth par on a reset date, so pricing = set the fixed bond's value equal to par.

06Currency & equity swaps

Currency swaps

  • Notionals are usually exchanged at start and end. Payments are in different currencies, so they are not netted. Each leg can be fixed or floating.
  • Pricing solves three unknowns: one notional and two fixed rates. Each fixed rate uses F11 with that currency's own discount factors.
F13

NAa​=S0​×NAb​

S0 quoted as units of currency a per 1 b.

= value of bond in a − St × value of bond in b, in currency a. Use the current spot rate St and each currency's current discount factors. The receive-b party's value is −VCS.

Equity swaps

  • Equity leg cash flow = NAE × equity return (with or without dividends, per the contract). The equity leg can be negative, so the equity payer may receive.
  • Receive-fixed, pay-equity = long a fixed bond + short equity. Use it to cut equity exposure without selling (tax, liquidity).
  • Fixed rate: same as F11. Pay-floating or equity-for-equity swaps need no pricing (the floater prices at par; the fixed legs cancel).

07Exam traps

⚠️
  • Direction of arbitrage: forward too high → carry (buy spot with borrowed money, sell forward). Too low → reverse carry (short spot, lend, buy forward).
  • Timing of carry benefits: a coupon or dividend received later has a smaller FV, so the forward price is higher. A newly announced dividend cuts Ft and the long's value.
  • Rates rise → Ft rises → long forward gains, short forward loses. Pay-fixed swaps and long FRAs also gain.
  • Futures value after mark-to-market = 0, not the price change.
  • Accrued interest uses days since the last coupon, not days to the next. Don't forget to subtract AIT or to use the exponent T in FV.
  • FRA: discount the settlement by 1 + Lmtm (paid at h). For value at g, find the new FRA rate for the same expiry, then discount over T − g.
  • Swap rate: numerator = 1 − last PV factor; denominator includes every factor. Annualise with 1/AP.
  • Swap value: multiply the rate difference by ΣPV of the remaining payments and the notional. Use the current swap rate, not an average of spot rates.
  • Currency swaps: payments aren't netted, and value uses the current spot rate St. Check which currency is "a" from how S is quoted.
  • Equity swap value: the equity leg is NAE × St/St−1 (since the last reset). If the equity rises and rates don't fall, the receive-fixed side is negative.
